Privacy statement

Introduction

The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador maintains this website to help you obtain information about its programs and services. We’re committed to protecting the privacy of individuals and organizations who visit this website, in compliance with the Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act, 2015 (ATIPPA, 2015).

A departmental website may have a different privacy statement, depending on the nature of its site. You should review a website’s privacy statement each time you submit personal information.

General Information Collected

Our web server automatically collects and saves a limited amount of information when you visit gov.nl.ca. This information includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (e.g. 123.45.67.890)
  • Browser type and version (e.g. Google Chrome, Apple Safari)
  • Page(s) visited
  • Date and Time of page(s) visited

Definition of personal information

Personal information means recorded information about an identifiable individual, including the individual’s name, address or telephone number. ATIPPA, 2015 outlines several examples of personal information: www.assembly.nl.ca/Legislation/sr/statutes/a01-2.htm

Collecting personal information

The only identifiable personal information collected by this website is information you give voluntarily (e.g. using a government online service or submitting a complaint, comment or feedback electronically). Government considers the personal information you provide through this website confidential. This information will only be used for purposes authorized by ATIPPA 2015.

If you choose not to use the Internet to provide personal information through this website, you may still be able to access government services by telephone, fax, mail, or in person. Not all options may be available in every situation.

Security

Government is obligated to protect your personal information by making reasonable security arrangements against such risks as unauthorized access, collection, use, disclosure or disposal. Security measures are integrated into the design, implementation and daily operating practices as part of Government’s ongoing commitment to protecting your personal information.

In addition to these general security practices, we also take extra steps to safeguard information collected through web analytics tools.

Links to other websites

This website may include links to websites operated by other public and/or private sector organizations. The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Cookies

When you visit or browse a website, a small piece of data may be sent from the website and stored on your computer. You can adjust browser settings to deny cookies or to warn you when a web site is about to place a cookie on your hard drive.

The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador websites may use cookies in a limited capacity to collect anonymous information used towards enhancing your browsing experience.

Web Analytics

In addition to the general information described above, the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador uses web analytics tools to help understand how people use our websites and to make them better for visitors.

What is Web Analytics?

Web analytics means looking at data about how people use our websites. We use tools like Google Analytics and Meta Insights (from Facebook) to see things like which pages people visit and how long they stay. These tools only collect information that cannot be used to identify you personally. All the data is grouped together and made anonymous.

What Information Do We Collect for Analytics?

When you visit a Government of Newfoundland and Labrador website, we may collect these types of information for analytics:

  • The type of browser, computer, or phone you use
  • The city, province, or country you are in
  • How you got to our website (for example, from a search engine or social media)
  • Which pages you look at and how long you stay
  • How far you scroll and what you click on
  • If you watch videos, we see if you play or pause them
  • If you fill out a form, we see if you finish it
  • What words you search for on our site
  • How people interact with our social media (like total likes or shares—not who liked or shared)

We use cookies and other digital markers to remember your preferences (like language or location) and to help with analytics. You can change your browser settings to block cookies, but some website features might not work if you do.

Important: We do not collect or keep your IP address using Google Analytics or Meta Insights. IP addresses are only used for a short time to figure out your general location and are deleted right away. Our web servers may record IP addresses for security reasons, but we do not use them for marketing or to build profiles about you.

Collection is authorized under section 61(c) of the ATIPPA (2015)

Why Do We Collect this Information?

We use this information to:

  • Make our websites work better for everyone
  • See how well our online ads and campaigns are doing
  • Share content that matches what people are interested in

Sometimes, we work with Google and Meta to help us with analytics. These companies may store the grouped, anonymous data outside Canada. They must follow strict privacy and security rules.

If you use social media platforms like Facebook to connect with us, remember that these sites are not run by the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador. Check their privacy policies to see how they handle your information. We only use information from social media in a grouped way (for example, total likes or shares).

How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador ensures our web analytics partners use strong security measures to protect your data including meeting industry security standards and certifications. We also use software to watch for any attempts to break into our websites.

Your Privacy Choices

You can choose not to be tracked by:

  • Blocking cookies in your browser settings
  • Using browser add-ons like the https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

To manage how Meta (Facebook) collects analytics data, please check your privacy settings on Facebook or Instagram, or visit Meta’s privacy resources for guidance.

Contact Information

Please direct any questions regarding this privacy policy or ATIPPA, 2015 to the Access to Information and Protection of Privacy (ATIPP) Office.

If you have a complaint or concern about your personal information, please contact the relevant public body. If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction ATIPPA, 2015 provides a mechanism for independent review through the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner.
